Step 3

Make Your $600 Monthly Decision

You do not have to invest all $600. Spending money on current wants is allowed. Your job is to understand the tradeoff.
Remember the tax difference: For this scenario, treat the 401(k) as traditional: contributions generally go in before federal income tax, the money grows tax-deferred, and withdrawals in retirement are generally taxed as income. A Roth IRA uses money that has already been taxed; qualified withdrawals in retirement are generally tax-free. Neither is automatically “better”—your job is to explain why your choice makes sense.
